Death Star

A procedural model with a superlaser dish, an equatorial trench, and shading computed per pixel. Rendered live in the browser.

Rendered at up to 420 px with a ray-marched surface. Pauses when scrolled off-screen, and shows a still frame if your system requests reduced motion.
