About

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with a major in Information Systems. That combination lets me work on both sides of a project: understanding what the business needs and how the technology will deliver it.

My day-to-day focus is requirements gathering, structured analysis, process mapping, and current-state versus target-state design. I work with SQL and Power BI for data and reporting, Azure DevOps for delivery, and SAP and other ERP systems for enterprise processes. I also build with React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and TypeScript, so I can speak to developers in their own terms.

I am looking for business analyst, systems analyst, technical business analyst, IT consultant, or DevOps-adjacent roles.
